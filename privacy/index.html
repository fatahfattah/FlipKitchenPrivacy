Privacy policy

Nothing leaves your iPhone

Last updated: 25 October 2026

FLIP Kitchen does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your cravings and recipes. Recipes are written by Apple Intelligence's on-device model on your iPhone. What you type is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your kitchen profile. Your allergies, favourite cuisines and disliked ingredients are saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone, and used only to write and check recipes.
  • Your cookbook. Recipes you keep are saved on your iPhone only.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Microphone and speech recognition

If you turn on voice commands, the app listens for short commands like "next step" while you cook. It uses Apple's Speech framework and asks for on-device recognition, which is used whenever your iPhone supports it. If on-device recognition isn't available, Apple may process the audio under Apple's privacy policy. FLIP Kitchen never records, saves or sends your audio.

Purchases

Subscriptions to FLIP Kitchen Pro are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks whether your subscription is active on your iPhone, using Apple's StoreKit. To count your two free recipes, the app keeps a small counter in your iPhone's Keychain; it never leaves the device. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles purchases.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules alerts on your iPhone for timers you start. Nothing is sent to a server.

Children

FLIP Kitchen does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with a new date at the top.
