Support

How can we help?

Email weryaba@hotmail.com and we'll get back to you. The answers below cover the most common questions.

Which iPhones does it work on?

FLIP Kitchen writes recipes with Apple Intelligence, so it needs an iPhone that supports it: iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, every iPhone 16 (including iPhone 16e), and newer iPhones, including iPhone 17, iPhone Air and iPhone Duo. It needs iOS 27 or later.

The app asks me to turn on Apple Intelligence

Open Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri and turn on Apple Intelligence, then come back to the app. The app has a button that takes you straight to Settings.

It says the model is downloading

After Apple Intelligence is turned on, your iPhone downloads its model in the background. This usually takes a few minutes on Wi-Fi. Keep the app open, or come back later: it opens by itself once the model is ready.

How long does a recipe take?

Usually 20 to 40 seconds. The recipe is written on your iPhone, so it works without a connection and nothing you type leaves the device.

Free recipes and FLIP Kitchen Pro

Your first two recipes are free. After that, FLIP Kitchen Pro writes unlimited recipes for $5.00 a month or $50.00 a year (prices may differ by country; the App Store shows your local price). Your cookbook, timers and voice commands stay free, and recipes you've kept can always be cooked again.

Cancelling or changing your plan

Open Settings > FLIP Kitchen Pro > Manage subscription in the app, or Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone. You can switch between monthly and yearly there, or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period; you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

I subscribed but the app still shows the paywall

Tap Restore on the paywall, or Settings > FLIP Kitchen Pro > Restore purchases in the app. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with. If it's still not working, email us.

Refunds

Payments go through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Allergies and disliked ingredients

Set them on first launch, or any time in Settings > Your kitchen. Every recipe is written around them and checked for your allergies before you see it. If a recipe still contains an allergen after a second attempt, the app won't show it.

Recipes are written by AI and can contain mistakes. Always check ingredient labels if you have an allergy.

Using it on iPhone Duo

  • Unfolded: your ingredients on one side, the current step on the other.
  • Tabletop: half-fold it and set it down. The step stands up and the timer sits on the counter.
  • Folded: the outer screen picks up where you left off, timers included.

On other iPhones, turn the phone sideways for the same two-pane view.

Timers

Tap Fire on a step's timer to start it. Allow notifications when asked, so a timer can alert you when the app isn't open. You can minimise a running timer to the edge of the screen and keep cooking. To change notification permission later, go to Settings > Notifications > FLIP Kitchen.

Voice commands

Tap the microphone key while cooking and say "next step", "previous step", "repeat", "start timer" or "how much garlic?". The app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time.

Deleting your data

Everything (your kitchen profile and cookbook) is stored only in the app on your iPhone. Delete a recipe from the cookbook to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything. See the privacy policy.
